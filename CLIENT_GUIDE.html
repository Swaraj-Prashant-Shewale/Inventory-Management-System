Your Inventory System — Getting Started

A short guide to signing in and checking your stock, from any phone, tablet or computer.

There is nothing to install. Your inventory lives on a secure website — open the link, sign in, and your stock is right there. This guide walks you through it in five simple steps.

1 Open the website and sign in

Open this link in any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge — on a phone or computer):

🔗  https://your-inventory-address-here

Enter the username and temporary password that were given to you when your account was set up. Tap Sign in.

Tip: On a phone, use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” option to keep the site one tap away, like an app.

2 Choose your own password (first time only)

The first time you sign in, the system asks you to replace the temporary password with your own private one. This is so that only you know it.

  • Type the temporary password once more in Current password.
  • Choose a new password — at least 8 characters, and not something obvious like all numbers or the word “password”.
  • Type it again to confirm, then tap Change password.
Keep it private. Nobody — not even the person who set up your account — can see your new password. If you ever forget it, ask them to reset it for you.

3 Your Home screen

After signing in you land on Home. Along the top you’ll see quick numbers:

ItemsHow many products are in your catalogue. Tap it to open your stock list.
Open purchase / sales ordersOrders still in progress.
Stock valueThe total value of what you’re holding. (Shown to owners and managers only.)

Below that is Reminders — the system watches your stock for you and posts a note here automatically when something needs attention, such as an item running low or a batch nearing expiry.

4 Check your stock any time

Tap Inventory in the menu (or use the search box at the top) to see every product and how much you have. You can:

  • Search by product name, code (SKU) or barcode.
  • Filter by warehouse if you have more than one location.
  • Tick “Below minimum only” to instantly see just the items running low.

What the columns mean:

On HandHow many units you physically have.
AvailableOn Hand minus anything already set aside for orders.
MinYour minimum level. When On Hand drops to or below this, the row turns red and a reminder appears on Home.
Avg Cost / ValueThe average cost and total value of the item. (Owners and managers only.)
Supplier / LocationWho you buy it from, and where it’s stored.

5 Sign out & stay secure

  • Tap Sign out (top-right of the menu) when you’re done, especially on a shared device.
  • For your safety, the system also signs you out automatically after about an hour of no activity — just sign back in.
  • Everyone who uses the system should have their own login. Please don’t share your password. Ask your administrator to add a login for each staff member.
Your data is your own. Every company on this system is kept completely separate — no other business can see your stock, and you cannot see theirs.
